How the reports were counted

  • One id, one report. The ticket export comes in overlapping pages, so some reports appear twice. Counts are of unique ids.
  • The cause comes from the tenant, not from intake. Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form and on the channel the report came through (src/lib/kb/causes.ts). The “intake tagged as” column shows how often the two disagree.
  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. Their dates come out with day and month swapped.
  • hint: on booking-v3, the form’s hint says MM/DD/YYYY. People type month first, as told, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way. A parser fix does not reach these.
  • one-day-move: the reports the desk read as a one-day move. Their dates move by one day, or differ between two places for the same meeting. A month-first parser cannot do that, so they are not counted under parser here, even though their tenants are day-first.

What changes for the desk

  • Do not merge on subject alone for date reports: the same subject came from three different bugs.
  • Close each split ticket on its own fix. #4471 stays the record of what was merged.
